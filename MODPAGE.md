# Modular Muskets

## What it is

Pick a barrel, a stock, and a bracing, put them together, and get a musket that's yours. Barrels are where the fun is: light stuff on fire, knock mobs across the room, bounce shots off walls, or drop something with one sonic slug. Bracings handle the scope feel and reload. Augments push it even further into scoped rifle or block-busting cannon territory.

Changed your mind on a build? Toss the musket back in the station and it pops apart into its parts.

## Making a musket

1. Craft a Musket Station and some Musket Balls.

2. Craft (or find) a Barrel, a Stock, and a Bracing. The recipes unlock the second you pick up their materials.

3. Open the station, drop in your three parts (plus an optional augment, see below), grab your musket.

4. Sneak to aim, hold to reload, click to fire. Aim for the head, you deal more damage!


## Recipes

| Item | Recipe |
| --- | --- |
| **Musket Station** | 3 iron across the top, 6 planks filling the rest. |
| **Musket Ball** | Iron nugget + gunpowder. Gives you 6. |
| **Wooden Barrel** | 3 planks in a row, stick under the middle. |
| **Wooden Stock** | 3 planks in an L, stick top-left. |
| **Bracing** | Metal, stick, metal in a row. |

No recipe for the musket itself. It only exists once you build it at the station. Recipes are unlocked when you get materials!

## Barrels

8 to collect. Most are craftable off a Wooden Barrel plus a themed item. The two best are chest loot only. Percentages are the chance per chest.

| Barrel | Does | Get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wooden** | Your starting point. | 3 planks + stick |
| **Bone** | Slightly  better. | Wooden Barrel + bone |
| **Resin** | Sticky shots that slow the target. | Wooden Barrel + resin clump |
| **Blaze** | Sets things on fire, and is extra powerful. | Wooden Barrel + blaze rod, or nether fortress chests (**20%**) |
| **Breeze** | Knockback and pierces through mobs.| Wooden Barrel + breeze rod |
| **Prismarine** | Marks targets and ricochets off walls.| Wooden Barrel + prismarine shard, or buried treasure (**20%**) and shipwreck treasure (**20%**) |
| **Echo** | Sonic slug.| Ancient city chests only (**10%**) |
| **Shulker** | Headshots instantly reload the next round. | End city treasure chests only (**30%**) |

Blaze and Prismarine you can craft or find. Echo and Shulker are rare!

## Bracings

All crafted metal, stick, metal. They just change how the gun handles (reloading and stability).

- **Copper / Iron** cheap, but the scope drifts fast and reload is slower.
- **Golden** near-instant zoom, and the only one that speeds up your reload!
- **Diamond** accurate zoom.
- **Netherite** steadiest scope, tightest shot, and +1 magazine. Smith it from a Diamond Bracing.

Iron Bracing in dungeons (**16%**), Diamond Bracing in trial chamber reward chests (**20%**), Netherite Bracing in bastion treasure (**24%**), and a Wooden Stock in mineshaft chests (**14%**).

## Ammo

Craft Musket Balls, or find them. They show up 4 to 12 at a time in nether fortresses, buried treasure, shipwrecks, bastions, dungeons, trial chambers, mineshafts, desert pyramids, jungle temples, pillager outposts, and strongholds.

## Augments

Slot one of these vanilla items in the augment slot:

- **Spyglass** proper scope and dead accurate while aimed.
- **Comparator** 3-round burst.
- **Ender Chest** +3 magazine.
- **Heart of the Sea** shoots underwater.
- **Heavy Core** more damage, punches through blocks.
- **Totem of Undying** absorption on a kill.
- **Nether Star** more of everything: damage, mag, faster reload.

## Config

One file at `config/niche.json`, made on first launch. Edit it with the game closed. Scale recoil, change zoom, toggle scope sway, dial the vignette, or switch off all barrel abilities.
